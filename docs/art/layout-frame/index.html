What the code does today

From top to bottom the screen runs HUD, lift, message, bench, queue, then a toolbar with Scale and Restart. Send sits inside the lift panel, in the right column.

  • Send is the last tap of every trip and the hardest to reach. It sits about a quarter of the way down the screen, on the far side from the queue, where a right thumb has to stretch.
  • The easy bottom strip holds the rarest buttons. Scale and Restart own it. Restart costs a life and sits where a thumb rests, so a slip is expensive.
  • Every trip crosses the whole screen. Pick someone at the bottom, board at the top, then Send at the top right.

The proposal, with power options

Keep the lobby order. Move what you tap most to where the thumb already is, and give the bottom bar to the powers: four round buttons with their counts (every power starts at 2), then a wide Send.

  • Swap swaps the front residents of two lanes. Tap Swap, then the two lanes.
  • +2 seats adds two bench seats for this level.
  • +1 trip adds one lift trip.
  • Express (my reading of "send people by lift") delivers the selected resident straight to their floor, with no seat and no trip used. Tell me if you meant something else.
  • Scale stays, but moves onto the gauge. It previews the needle, so it sits on the gauge plate as a small button. Without it nothing ever teaches a player the weights, and the whole game is guessing them.
  • No rider count. Send is a plain wide button. Restart lives in the pause menu behind the gear, with a confirm.

Rules this reopens

Locked rule 9 says the Scale is the one booster, and the PRD must be reopened before any of this is built. Swap and Express are new ways to win a level, +2 seats and +1 trip change the jam and out-of-trips fails, and four consumables need prices and ad rewards through the ad policy. The generator and solver also need a pass so levels do not become trivial.
